# Veru (Trigwynn)

Discord `vrnk3014`. Plays Trigwynn, a duskwalker oracle of Pharasma posing as a wandering bard.
Also called Verča. Partner of Vojta; they play from the same room.

**In one line:** the quiet one who is bolder in fiction than anyone else at the table, least
fluent in the rules, most invested in secrets, and the only player who initiates dark beats
on her own.

## Who she is (from the recordings)

- Medical student. Anki decks from first to fourth year, hospital placements, IPVZ courses,
  a urology textbook, "první alkoholice, jsem dostala od pacienta" (21/10 03:55). She and Makak,
  a hospital doctor, bond and spar over medicine; he teases "to tvoje studium, to není práce"
  (29/09 02:18).
- Lives between a dorm in Prague with no kitchen (long complaint, 21/10 04:05) and lodgings in
  Pelhřimov that she made homely with fairy lights (29/09 02:40). Wants to host a live session.
- Not a tech person and says so. Six-year-old laptop with 6 MB free, computer freezing in
  three of four sessions, an hour of the 21/10 session lost to it.
- Said on 11/10 she will need to play less from November (02:41).
- Her character's name is an anagram found via Wikipedia, "nějaká norská mytologie" / Beowulf.
  She wrote six pages of backstory and a side plot she could not fit in (29/09 02:11, 02:33).

## How she talks

**Out of character:** short, fragmentary, fast. Signature fillers are "počkej" (often three in a
row while fighting Foundry), "fakt", "mega", "jakoby", "no", "jo". Gaming jargon is thin and
often wrong: "Forbidding Verge", "Sense Direction" for Sense Motive, "Pickle Lock", "hodím si na
hajt". She paraphrases mechanics in Czech instead ("to kouzlo, jak spojí dva lidi, jakože
potvorku a hráče"). Swears rarely and mildly ("sakra", "Oh damn", "Shit!"), with one sharp
exception ("Bud si hlady, pičo!", about a neighbour's dog, 21/10 01:22). Humour is a quick
sarcastic shrug: after falling face-first into the holy-water bowl, "To teda byla povinná akce"
(29/09 00:56).

**In character:** a different register entirely. Formal, feminine, polite "vy" to NPCs, slightly
stilted ("Cítíte se nyní už dost silný, abyste jsme se vydali na to zůstříc nebezpečí.", 21/10
03:08). To merchants a naive sales pitch ("Nechcete si koupit tady tuto šavli? Určitě je moc
dobrá na bojování.", 13/10 00:21). To Ezekiel after the beggar kill, cold and controlling:
"Už mě nikdy nesleduj." (13/10 00:56). She narrates Trigwynn in the third person a lot, and when
the GM gives her a sensation she extends it in her own words rather than just acknowledging it
("Trigwynn cítí, jak její tělo znovu dostává do sebe hmotnou podstatu…", 13/10 00:51).

She is in character perhaps a third of the time; much of her airtime goes to UI trouble and
"můžu se hodit na…?" questions.

## How she behaves at the table

- **Asks permission, constantly.** The least rules-fluent player and honest about it: "Já nevím,
  jestli jsem dobře pochopila to, co jste mi poslala do chatu, ale to bylo takový složitý."
  (21/10 02:39). Cannot find Diplomacy on the sheet, confused by coin conversion, did not know
  armour had to be set to Worn. Asks for take-backs ("No sakra, můžu to vzít zpátky?").
- **Bold in fiction regardless.** Volunteers to negotiate with kobolds, tries to steal the silver
  bowl, talks to a kobold mid-fight, wants to Coerce the last one, walks first into the A09
  corridor kicking everything. Three kills and two crits on 21/10 despite waiting for others to
  decide her turn ("mezi tím, co jsem meditovala, rozhodli jste něco, co teď budu dělat", 03:08).
- **Cautious analyst in puzzles, the group's conscience in social scenes.** On 11/10 she does the
  Crafting work on the machine, lays out the plan, warns about the trap three times, mediates the
  boys' scuffle in character ("měli byste se věnovat tady důležitým věcem a neseprát"), and
  argues for telling Tamily the truth. She wants to verify the riddle coin rather than trust it
  ("třeba nám kecá", 29/09 02:00).
- **She hunts, with planning.** The beggar kill on 13/10 was entirely hers. She lagged behind the
  group "a pozoruje ostatní lidi" before the GM placed anyone, then checked whether he could die
  a good death and how bad her own state was before acting. Two sessions earlier she had
  flagged it herself: "já jsem dost dlouho nenašla žádnou oběť" (11/10 00:43). No hesitation
  out of character, and she refused Makak's crude alternative ("Ne, počkej, to ne.").
- **Deliberate deception.** The fake-calluses plan was her idea ("tak se pokusím si prostě udělat
  na rukách nějaký mozoly", 21/10 03:22), followed by waving her hands where Arnold would notice.
  She chose shaming Arnold over saving Ezekiel ("Ne, tohle se mi líbí mnohem víc", 02:37) and
  justified it afterwards with first-aid scene-safety logic.
- **Under pressure she gets anxious and defensive, not compliant.** During the tech hour on
  21/10: "Já to fakt nedělám schválně.", "Já se fakt bojím, fakt to nic neudělá.", then "Teď se
  bojím, že jsem podepsala ortel smrti nad sebou." She refuses to share her password ("Ne, fakt
  ne.") and flatly rejects disk advice at the end ("Ne, ale já to vím… To nepotřebuji."). Yet
  when her PC froze mid-game on 11/10 she turned it into roleplay twice instead of stalling.
- **Mixed pride and embarrassment about the dark side.** When Makak calls her a psychopath:
  "Vůbec se mi nelíbí… Teď mě znervózňuješ… Jinak nejsem psychopat." (21/10 02:01), and half an
  hour later, "Takže já jsem ten psychopat." She gives guarded minimal answers in the question
  game and mutters "A jo, já jsem to neměla říkat" after one (03:43).
- **Time-aware.** First to ask how long people want to play and to say it is late.
- Reaction to success is understated ("To je tak crazy."). Reaction to failure is a shrug and
  another angle, not sulking. Persistent to the point of stubbornness in haggling (three pushes
  at Keeleno, then Carman twice, until he caved).

## Relationship to Trigwynn

Deeply invested in the curse and the secrecy, far more than in mechanics. In the private call
(26/10) she defined the side quest herself: Trigwynn wants to "rozseknout" whether she can exist
without being on the edge between death and humanity, either by freeing herself or by going
"full time smrtka", because living on the edge is exhausting. She explained the backstory at
length: a childhood in the monastery taking souls as a matter of course, the war that destroyed
it, and meeting the "bad death" as the moment she understood what she carries. She also thinks
ahead about how backstory should land: wants epic moments rather than "vystřílet náboje", and
suggested an oracle or a dream vision. Offered to feed Arnold visions about his own death and
"nenápadně" hint she is involved. She said Free Archetype scares her and that she waits for
level 2 "jako na spasení".

What she enjoys: roleplay stakes, intrigue, NPC negotiation, dark description. Combat and loot
barely register; she never raises loot herself.

## With the other players

- **Vojta:** a unit. He protects her in fiction ("drž se za někým"), she pokes at his loot and
  corrects him ("To jsem ti říkala teďka já a nejsem AI."). Minor friction when he did not back
  her haggling (13/10 00:24); he then did. She asks him for help against Arnold.
- **Makak:** the main teasing axis of the campaign (hands, hips, calluses). She answers in kind
  ("A kdybych měla mozoly?") with no sign of annoyance. He is also the one who offers her an
  external drive and help moving.
- **Milan:** the only genuine irritation on tape, when he pushed the healer's kit on her
  ("mě to otravuje", 13/10 00:02). Otherwise natural puzzle partners; Rikkit defers to her.
- **Dan:** needs the most support and resists it the most. Polite procedural questions in play;
  anxious and defensive when micromanaged on tech. Follows a lead well when the GM answers in
  character. Thanked him for the private call and said small sessions like that are welcome.

## What works with her

- Paraphrase mechanics in Czech and hand her options at the moment she needs them (as with Soul
  Siphon on 21/10). She will not go looking in the sheet herself.
- Give her a sensation or an image to extend; she builds on it well.
- Social scenes, secrets and moral dilemmas are where she shines. She will initiate dark beats;
  the GM's job is consequences, not prompting.
- Keep tech off the critical path. Pressure on the PC makes her anxious and the evening suffers.
- She expects less play from November; small private calls keep her attached.
