# Milan (Rikkit Klaw)

Discord `mj696969`. Plays Rikkit, a ratfolk inventor from another world who speaks broken Czech
in the third person. The only player who is always remote.

**In one line:** the most disciplined in-character voice at the table, the happiest about bad
dice, a helpful rules-explainer who is the worst with the VTT, and a father who leaves early
every session.

## Who he is (from the recordings)

- A parent with limited evenings. Leaves for "tatínkovské povinnosti" (29/09 01:53), runs down
  for a courier mid-combat (29/09 01:19), "Už fakt běžíme, mějte se" (11/10 02:12), "musím běžet
  lidi" (13/10 01:00). Absent on 21/10. Dropped into the Makak private call for six minutes on
  26/10 and left with "já musím mizet teďka".
- Runs his own soundboard that fights the GM's. Discovered he could see everyone's rolls through
  a permissions bug and reported it immediately (29/09 00:49).
- Some forms sound Slovak ("prsteň", "Dobré"); may be Whisper. Describes forging with what sounds
  like real knowledge ("pomalu vytepu ty kraje… zakalím to", 13/10 00:31). Makes a Bosch joke
  about artisan's tools.
- Likes the system. In the private-call cameo he defended PF2e's realism as "dobře zasazený do té
  mechaniky", said it makes sense after one explanation, and praised the three-action economy for
  pushing variety.

## How he talks

**In character:** the strictest voice at the table. Infinitives, third person, no conjugation,
heavy repetition, kept up even for long speeches: "Rikkit, Rikkit, mít tolik, tolik vzpomínek,
všude krysa, všude krysa, všichni bát…" (29/09 00:02); "Něco vždycky fungovat." (11/10 00:30);
"Paní říct, že mi zjistit, kdo ukrást ryba. Mě asi vědět, kdo ukrást ryba." (11/10 01:53);
"A děkovat, děkovat. Moc hodná." (13/10 00:59). He also narrates Rikkit's body: nail biting,
sniffing everything, holding Trigwynn's skirt, hiding behind Ezekiel's legs, squatting on a barrel.

**Out of character:** the most anglicised Czech at the table, fast and self-interrupting:
"nerfnout", "override-nout", "reloadnu", "vycraftím", "tinkeroval", "Overdrive", "once per round",
"anyway, anyway", "bro", "kámo", a tic of "Nice." (three times in one line). Mild swearing
("sakra", "shit", one "what the fuck"). Apologetic: "Sorry… Já tady taky nemám moc prostoru,
omlouvám se."

**Humour:** self-deprecating slapstick and Rikkit's naive literalism. "To je zrovna jediná barva,
kterou právě vidím, bro. Jak jsi tmavej." (he sees in black and white). "Já jsem Rikkit. Já napřed
dělám, potom přemýšlím." "Nalít krev na příště? Fungovat?"

## How he behaves at the table

- **Act-first tinkerer who still checks for consent.** Starts fiddling with the machine before
  anyone agrees ("Dobře, Rikkit už začal, pošlu screenshot."), wins the tug-of-war for the lever,
  makes the trap fire. Yet he holds the lever and reads Trigwynn's face with Sense Motive before
  pulling, and says "pořád čekám na ostatních potvrzení" (11/10 00:27).
- **Healthiest reaction to failure at the table.** Overdrive fails three times in one session,
  each time "Failure, OK, nevadí." A critical fail on a save gets a shrug; clearing it gets "yes,
  yes, dobré". "Aspoň něco." First initiative of 1: a joke.
- **Rules-helpful, VTT-clumsy.** Explains action economy for drawing items to others, explains the
  encounter dice, walks Veru through accepting a trade, "Aby to bylo mechanicky správně, teda tak
  já dám follow the expert skill." But rolls the wrong skill, breaks his initiative with a
  cancelled Delay (GM: "ty seš fakt rozbitej"), and does not know all the skill names.
- **Information discipline before being asked.** "Jo, jenom dělám roleplay, protože já to nevím,
  ještě tu informaci" (11/10 00:21), said before the GM made the same point, while the in-room
  trio leaked Crafting results freely.
- **Detail- and number-oriented.** Noticed the bolt price did not match and forced the currency
  ruling (13/10 00:14), asks Carman about deadline, materials and plans, double-checks that tools
  were really added to his sheet.
- **Loot- and gadget-first, sometimes pushy.** "Já to hned začnu rabovat, já vůbec nemám nějaký
  socialty". Pours his whole canteen into the sacred bowl for no stated reason, crafts a lockpick
  from his own bolts, grabs Carman's hammer uninvited, loads his crossbow to "být první" into the
  fight. Pressed the healer's kit on Trigwynn and then Ezekiel until Veru said it annoyed her.
- **Curious rather than brave.** Wants to bottle kobold blood "na příště", but "Bojím se jít sám."
- **Meta-reflective at times.** "Zajímavý fenomén… že něco trvá díl, jak to hraješ, než jako i dole
  v té hře." Suspects Keeleno's interest is "konkrétně na mě", so he is thinking about his origin.

## Relationship to Rikkit

Loves the inventor and craftsman fantasy and the physical comedy. Volunteers backstory in
character: rats everywhere, fear, "dávat pozor", tools "něco zapomenout doma? Nevím, jak vrátit",
does not know local money. His stance on moral questions is mercenary in character ("Práce. Víc
práce, víc peněz.") but he voted with Trigwynn for honesty with Tamily. Enjoys crafting,
loot-as-tools, NPC deals and jokes more than combat; happily swaps to a sling when out of bolts;
forgets NPC names.

## With the other players

- **Vojta:** commander and specialist. Ezekiel gives him the engineering job and public trust;

- **Dan:** asks him to adjudicate ("řekni motiv DM-kovi, ale ten to rozhodne"), takes teasing well
  ("Jseš jistě na záchodě"), triggered the currency ruling with a precise question.

## What works with him

- Put his scenes early. He leaves around the one-to-two-hour mark almost every time, and asks
  the GM to position his character for next session.
- Crafting, gadgets and NPC deals are his fun; a workshop hook or a commission from Carman beats
  a fight.
- He is already thinking about why Keeleno watches him. His origin is a hook ready to pull.
- Consent-based play suits him: he waits for confirmation before pulling levers if given the
  chance.
- VTT help is needed; rules help he can give.
