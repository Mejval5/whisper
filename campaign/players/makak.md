# Makak (Arnold)

Discord `makak4376`, real name Marek. Plays Arnold, an amnesiac android gunslinger who is a
Schwarzenegger reference. Addresses Dan as "Magne" (on four separate sessions, so a real
nickname, not a transcription error).

**In one line:** the rules-literate deadpan tactician and loot hound of the group table, who
one-on-one turns out to be a candid, swearing, overloaded guy who sees himself as the group's
main roleplayer and yields to the GM faster than his arguing suggests.

## How he talks

**At the group table:** deadpan, dry, flat delivery, complete and often technical sentences.
Prefaces questions with "Dotaz," or "Mám dotaz?". Filler "tyjo", trailing "no". Narrates Arnold
in third-person stage directions ("Arnold hlídá dveře", "Arnold si brousí meč", "Arnold si hraje
se zbraní") and slides into and out of character without marking it. Heavy English rules
vocabulary inside Czech sentences ("stride", "encumbered", "bulk 7,2", "improvised attack",
"main handou", "take cover", "GG"). Heaviest swearer at the table ("ty vole", "do prdele", "na
hovno", "debilní", "píču", "So fucking close."). References other games (Skyrim NPCs, Divinity).

**Arnold's machine voice** comes and goes. He announced dropping it on 29/09 ("tak já začnu
mluvit normálně už, tyjo"), it was nearly absent on 11/10 and 13/10 (strongman slapstick
instead), and it was back in full as the group's favourite bit on 21/10: "Cítím problém, cítím
problém, kritický error, nevím jak postupovat dál.", "Mé tělo neprodukuje sliny.", "Najít,
zabít, zlikvidovat.", "Tvůj argument přijímám.", "I'll be back!" Treat it as a comedic instrument
he reaches for when the scene rewards it, not a fixed character voice.

**Humour:** deadpan literalism and heckling. Takes 8 damage: "Ahoj." Offers to carry the merchant
so he need not walk. Live meta-commentary as a note-taker: "Píšu si poznámku, Trigwynn se snaží
zabít bezdomovce." Self-deprecating running bit about dice: "Toto je šesté sezení a v každém
sezení dostanu kritku", "Já jsem Disco Koule." Escalates a gag until it breaks (potion into the
skull, then mouth-to-mouth with the potion, then fingers down the throat).

**One-on-one (26/10):** a different person. Long, run-on, associative sentences driven by "jako"
in nearly every one, "a co ještě?" to himself, swearing roughly doubled ("píčovina" five times),
no in-character voice at all, and he admits "se blbě jako vysvětluje" when a third person joins.

## How he behaves at the table

- **Logistician and tactician.** Organises healing before moving, proposes a leader and a
  marching order twice, barricades doors, blocks the storeroom, guards doors during the shrine
  scene instead of playing with the water, holds his turn via Delay after checking the rule.
  Tracks bolts, bulk, who carries what; insists two people carry the oil and refuses to pay for
  it; buys 80 bolts so he can supply Rikkit and five torches because you can fight with them.
- **Rules-literate where it benefits Arnold, vague elsewhere.** Corrects the GM on item value, coin
  conversion, crit threshold, ancestry vs archetype, the level of Turn Back the Clock; found the
  missing Take Cover and Earn Income actions in Foundry; knows his gunslinger kit. But did not
  know you can stop mid-Stride, how bulk or containers work, what the Repair Toolkit he bought
  does, and misread low-light vision as darkvision.
- **Argues, then yields fast.** Argues numbers and consistency with the GM ("Ty koboldi ty praky a
  kuličky mají, nebo to je nějaký glitch?"), blames the text when a misunderstanding happens
  ("Ty jsi to napsal, Magne, mě se nedívej."), but once a ruling is firm he backs off within a
  line: "Jo, už to chápu. Jo, sorry.", "Já se musím přivyknout na jiný způsoby každé hry."
  One-on-one he argues once with a concrete counter-proposal and never escalates.
- **Frustration is about the gunslinger's action economy, not the group.** On 11/10 he went down
  in round one, called the draw-item rules "úplně debilní", got the GM's one sharp line of the
  night ("nebuď tak negativní, do prdele"), and ended the debrief with "Takže jsem useless." He
  still did everything asked (barricade, dropped the two-sword idea). On 26/10 he framed it as
  "chápu, že to chceš založit na realismu, ale zas ať toho realismu tam není tolik, že to pak
  kazí plynulost" and said there is no blocker.
- **Impulsive in fiction, loot-first.** Punches the machine, bursts through the door the moment
  Ezekiel says "pojďme" and eats a crit, raises his sword at a merchant "chtěl jsem ukázat
  ostrost meče". Palmed all eight riddle coins while roleplaying "dovolte, ať ochráním skupinu"
  ("Už jsou u mě v baťůžku."), sells a pile of six items in one breath, wants the kobold armour
  dragged out in the net, reads token names in the VTT for hints. "Easy shit, to koupím."
- **Multitasks when bored.** Emails and car insurance during the 21/10 tech hour, Windows setup
  during the whole private call. Puzzles bore him outright ("Arnolda puzzle nezajímá, ale
  zkontroluje ruce Trigwynn."). Engaged the moment there is a target.
- **Reads the other players aloud.** "A jo, vlastně on je očitý svědek toho, že jsi někoho zabila,
  už to chápu." Guessed the GM wrote Verrick's backstory two hours before Dan confirmed it.
  Defends Arnold's weirdness when the group turns on him: "Jednou se podíval na ruce, no bože.
  Proti Trigwynn, která tady zabíjí…" Wants an all-truth session: "tohle se nedá" (21/10 04:02).
- **Ruthless pragmatism in character.** When told the man Arnold robbed might recognise him: "v tom
  případě by ho Arnold radši zabil" (26/10 01:15). "Zabiju každého, kdo tady bude."
- Reaction to success is minimal ("No, Critical Success, nice."); to failure, a shrug and a stat.

## Relationship to Arnold

Dan admits he pushed Makak into the character (26/10 00:38). Backstory was four lines by design
and he has not read all the flashbacks ("nestihl jsem si to přečíst"). What he wants Arnold to be
is a straight Terminator: metal endoskeleton showing when flesh is blown off ("odlítávalo jako
maso z těla… byly tam vidět ty elektrické součástky, to by se mi líbilo hodně"), better at
passing as human than other androids, one-liners, a T-1000-style rival ("žádnýho Kailary, já chci
nějaký T-1000"). Proposed fake visions that make him look like a traitor, a short-circuit
berserk mode "začne terminátořit… dokud ho nerestartujou", being sent back by a current party
member, a group trip to the future. He limits himself deliberately to adding circumstances, not
deciding who Arnold was, because "já o tom teďka v tenhle moment nemám vědět" (26/10 00:52).

Build: delays Chronoskimmer until the time-travel reveal, takes Sentinel for AC meanwhile.
Primary in-game goal is a pistol ("sehnat někde pořádný zbraně, ten zbytek se nějak najde sám");
backup plan is to have Rikkit build one.

He sees himself as the group's main roleplayer and is frustrated that Veru and Vojta "roll-call"
instead of roleplay (26/10 00:37: "zjišťuju, že jsem jediný, kdo to dělá"). The roleplay he means
is cinematic, not conversational. Opinions on the others are blunt: characters "na půl useless",
"ten Ezekiel prostě chcípne vždycky", Rikkit had a repair kit "ale on to nikomu neřek".

The Arnold-eyes-Trigwynn thread is his longest-running bit: her thigh muscles on 29/09, the
hands on 11/10, two callus checks on 13/10, "Odhal svoje boky, prosím." on 21/10.

## What works with him

- He needs a "cool thing" soon: the pistol, a light source that does not cost a hand, a combat
  set piece with cinematic damage on Arnold. Most of his sourness traces to the action economy.
- State rulings firmly with a one-line why. He argues the number, not the authority, and drops
  it once the why is on the table.
- He keeps notes and remembers earlier rulings, so inconsistencies will be called out.
- He wants to be surprised about his own origin and is disciplined about not knowing. Keep the
  reveal his; give him circumstances (the crater, the robbed man, a rival) to play with.
- He is under real-life pressure right now. A private call where he can vent for ten minutes
  before the game talk works, as 26/10 showed.
