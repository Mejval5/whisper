# Vojta (Ezekiel Monstrans)

Discord `prkno`, nickname Prkno. Plays Ezekiel, a skeleton thaumaturge who hides behind a mask
and drinks from his hand (the chalice). Partner of Veru; they play from the same room.

**In one line:** the de facto party leader and the most in-character player by a wide margin,
a dry formalist who wants pace, stakes and drama, and who hands the spotlight away on purpose.

## Who he is (from the recordings)

- Software or data background. A job interview on statistics and modelling went badly on 29/09
  ("připravoval jsem se asi špatně", 02:43) and he half-jokingly asked Dan for a job at his
  company. Makak says on 26/10 he is "na brigádách a nemá teďka co dělat". Makes optimisation
  and AI jokes, checks rules with Gemini at the table (21/10 03:15).
- Played a previous campaign with Dan and Makak (the maze: "PTSD v bludišti"), knew the coin
  riddle from it and said so before solving it.
- Goes to a Warhammer Fantasy LARP as a skaven (29/09 02:20). Makes coffee late and gets
  forgiven for it. Mentions his mother's struggle with an old laptop. Plays via a phone-on-laptop
  Discord setup that once knocked him out of Foundry.
- Ezekiel's name is "monstrance" ("On je jen monstrance. Ale má to smysl."). He had two joke
  characters and picked the third, "normal" one.

## How he talks

**In character:** elevated, archaic, imperative Czech, delivered as complete rhetorical
sentences, often with a punchline he visibly prepares. "Nože, vyražme na cestu. Avšak, Trigwynn,
možná se drž za někým." (29/09 00:27). "Nedoporučuji to. A teď to zakazuji." (11/10 00:33).
"Neboť oba sdílíme tajemství spojené se smrtí, jichž pochopení není mezi lidmi časté…" (13/10
00:56). He typically narrates out of character first ("Ezekiela to hodně nasralo") and then
delivers the line in voice. Nearly every social beat gets an Ezekiel line; even logistics
("Přej mi ode mě 20 šípů.") come in register. Formal address of party members by full name.

**Out of character:** fast, plain, full of English and gaming jargon used correctly: "raise
shield", "Exploit Vulnerability", "Demoralize", "GG", "Nice. Mega nice.", "Well, ok. A jak to
udělám v UI-ku?", "sus", "Bro.". Swears in English ("Oh shit", "What the fuck, ne!", "that's
metal as fuck") and only occasionally in Czech ("ty vole", "Prdele").

Humour is dry and built on Ezekiel's undead nature: "Beru si to osobně" when centuries of wear
are mentioned; "Tak to lidé dělají." picking the biggest bed; "Takový žaludek, jako máš, ty bych
chtěl mít. A to myslím doslova."; "Sebou ve vaku nosíš čolky?" Dark callbacks too: the tech
crisis "možná takhle se cítil ten bezdomovec na ulici, akorát zrychleně" (21/10 01:15).

## How he behaves at the table

- **Leads, in character.** Gives orders ("Rikkite, jednej.", "Trigwynn, postaraj se o něj. Já
  budu držet stráž.", "zde nahoře, zadržím ho, postarejte se o zbytek"), bids for the spokesman
  role explicitly ("Přenechte tedy tento úkol mně… Vyhrožování je také forma vyjednávání.",
  29/09 00:41), accepts Keeleno's contract for the party, declines Carman's offer on Rikkit's
  behalf without asking, hosts and referees the question game and closes it. Refocuses the table
  after tangents: "co bylo posledním problémem, co jsme řešili? Vrátíme se z odbočky na hlavní
  misi." (21/10 01:10).
- **Investigates diegetically.** Follows tracks, asks precise follow-ups, re-lays the warding
  bones after disturbing them, delivers full theories and closes with "Vyražme." Interrogates the
  merchant like a lawyer ("Máš k tomu nějaký důkaz?").
- **Rules: competent and deferential.** Knows his class better than expected ("Spíš já bych měl
  mít ten dubious knowledge, ne?"), asks precise questions, self-corrects ("ne, minuta je deset
  kol"), accepts rulings without arguing, "Mám se to updatovat?", "To je moudré." Weak on Foundry
  UI (how to sell, how money works, where the Raise Shield button is). Honest when lost: "Vůbec
  nevím, co házím."
- **Story over dice.** Offered a Stealth roll, he says "Ne, dobrý, tak to zahrajeme tak, že mě
  viděla." (13/10 00:52). Asks to roll death saves privately with physical dice "Třeba pak mě to
  přijde taky napínavější" (21/10 02:19). During the tech hour he plays Two Steps from Hell and
  narrates it as a trailer with Makak.
- **Wants pace.** Pushed twice on 11/10 to do downtime by message and "co nejdřív došel do sklepa
  a clear'nul ho"; the GM declined. His impatience is with indecision, not with roleplay: he
  stepped back from the puzzle on purpose because "to není moje expertíza a spíš je to jako
  Rikkitova" (11/10 02:23).
- **Risk: moderate, curious, deliberate.** Prefers ambush to open fight, proposes the retreat and
  barricade, front-lines and takes hits, but chooses Demoralize and shield over extra attacks.
  Dips his rotting hand in the holy water and then pours it under his mask after seeing what it
  did to Trigwynn.
- **Protective and generous.** Restrains Arnold physically twice (grapple at the machine, sword at
  the merchant), drags downed Arnold back and shuts the door, gives Trigwynn 20 arrows, offers to
  Aid her haggling after she complained, covers for her lie to Arnold.
- **Lies well in character,** rolls Deception himself, and keeps secrets as a tease ("Odpověď je
  složitá, ale mohu snad prozradit, že to bylo mým přičiněním."). Yet he spontaneously spent his
  own secret to bond with Trigwynn in the alley.
- Reaction to failure is light ("GG! Asi ne.", "Ty jo, no tak jsem skončil."). First kill was
  celebrated. Eats a sugared lemon during breaks, announces bathroom breaks, otherwise the most
  on-task player.

## Relationship to Ezekiel

Strongly invested in his dignity, formality and the mask secret ("byl bych rád, abyste mi
všichni slíbili, že moje tajemství neopustí tuto skupinu", 29/09 00:26). Drops backstory hints
unprompted (interrogation training, "studoval jsem ezoteriku", a homeland where not haggling is an
insult). Precious about his sickle's handle. Treats Arnold's potion scene as a real in-fiction
grievance and stages the oil handover as a ritual. Theory-crafts for ten minutes how to make a
thaumaturge tank and concludes "není to optimalizovaný úplně na boj."

What he enjoys: deduction, dialogue, leadership, tension and drama. Combat is a duty done
competently and without relish. Loot rarely interests him.

## What works with him

- Give him stakes and suspense: private death saves, dramatic reveals, consequences. He wants to
  be scared and moved more than rewarded.
- Let him lead, but watch for him speaking for others (Rikkit's job offer, Tamily). A quiet NPC
  question aimed at another player breaks that.
- Keep town play moving; he tolerates it but counts the minutes to the dungeon.
- He will hand the spotlight to a specialist if the scene is clearly theirs. Make that explicit.
- Praise lands. So does a rules answer delivered as a one-liner he can use.
