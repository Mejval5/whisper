# Robo (Verrick)

Discord `robo_cuties`. Played Verrick, a human ranger. Left the game for good after 29/09/2026.
One session on tape, so this is a sketch.

**In one line:** an efficient combat player with no visible attachment to story, who left
decisions to others and the character to the GM.

## On tape

- Spoke little, almost entirely in mechanical shorthand: "Použije Hunt Prey, tím pádem má zdarma
  Recall Knowledge", "Dám Demoralize. Ok, konec tahu.", "zase ho rozsekne vejpůl. Easy. A může
  začít lootění. Yes." Several Slovak-looking forms ("Pekne", "Dobré", "zlomit nohu"); Whisper may
  be mangling him. No swearing recorded. Light video-game humour: "Verrick udělal poslední selfíčko
  s Octopusem a je připraven odejít."
- **Action-first, overruled.** "slyšeli jste to, pojďme to prozkoumat… kdo je jako první" was
  shut down by Arnold's heal-first order; he kicked the cell door before anyone considered tools;
  volunteered to carry the light because he fights one-handed.
- **Engaged in combat, passive outside it.** Two kobolds cut in half, Demoralize, Hunt Prey used
  correctly. No theory-crafting, no questions about the fiction, left the riddle to others:
  "Verrick to v tuto chvíli nechává na vás, týme." Needed prompting from the GM to take the orb.
- **Thin character by his own admission.** Dan wrote the backstory; Robo improvised on the spot
  ("řekněme půl roku, nepamatuji, no, ale musím ještě vymyslet proč"), which already contradicted
  the GM's lore. Makak guessed two hours earlier that the GM had written it.
- Left at 1:53 with "Zjistíme se příště. Díky, ahoj."
