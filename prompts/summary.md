# Session summary template

Write in Czech. Use character names (from campaign/speakers.json), not Discord usernames.
Whisper sometimes mishears names: fix them using campaign/glossary.txt and campaign/notes.md.
Where one Discord track carries two players (see notes.md), work out who spoke from context.

## What to keep and what to drop

The summary is the campaign's memory. Anything that could matter in a later session goes in,
however small. Read the whole in-game part of the transcript, not just the big scenes.

**Keep, every time:**

- Every piece of information the GM gives: room descriptions, clues, tracks, sounds, smells,
  what a Recall Knowledge / Seek / Sense Motive revealed, visions and flashbacks, lore dumps.
- Every decision the party makes, including small ones: which way they go and which they skip,
  marching order, who carries the light, who leads, whom they trust, what they refuse.
- Theories the characters voice about what is going on, even wrong ones, with who said them.
- Everything a character reveals about themselves in play (backstory, secrets, questions asked
  and answered or refused), and promises made between characters.
- Item movements: found, taken, traded, used up, given away. Who holds what at the end.
- Mechanical state that carries over: conditions, blessings, damage taken, resources spent.
- GM rulings and house rules that will apply again.
- Table decisions that shape the campaign: next session date and format, backstory
  agreements, anything the GM or players say about characters' future.

**Drop:**

- Foundry / VTT troubleshooting, finding buttons, dragging tokens, trade windows, audio problems.
- Rules explanations that didn't change what happened.
- Real-life chatter unrelated to the game.

If something is unclear in the audio, write it with `[?]` instead of guessing.
If something said at the table contradicts campaign/notes.md or the GM's lore, say so.

## Structure

### Shrnutí

3-6 sentences: what happened this session.

### Průběh

Chronological, grouped by scene or room. Each bullet starts with an approximate [HH:MM:SS]
timestamp. Keep the small beats inside their scene (a clue, a line of dialogue, a failed roll
that mattered).

### Stopy a informace

Everything the party learned, one bullet each: what, how they learned it, and who knows it.

### Rozhodnutí a teorie

Decisions the party made (and alternatives they rejected), plus theories the characters voiced.

### Postavy a NPC

Per character and NPC: what was revealed or changed about them this session.

### Kořist, předměty a stav družiny

Items and money (who took what), XP, conditions, blessings, damage, resources used.

### Mimo hru, ale důležité

GM rulings, house rules, schedule, backstory agreements. Skip the section if there is nothing.

### Otevřené otázky a plány

Unresolved threads, promises, what the party plans next, and contradictions to resolve.

### Hlášky

2-5 memorable quotes, with speaker.
